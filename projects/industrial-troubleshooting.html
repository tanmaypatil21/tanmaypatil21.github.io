PROJECT 04 / Mid-West Forge

Tracing faults across mechanical and controls systems

Combined PLC logic tracing, electrical diagnosis, and robot maintenance to investigate production equipment faults.

Hands-on equipment support
ENGINEERING AREAS

TOOLS & METHODS

01 / THE PROBLEM

What needed to change.

Production equipment issues can originate in wiring, controls, drives, or mechanical components. The symptom alone does not identify the source.

02 / MY CONTRIBUTION

My part in the work.

Used RSLinx and RSLogix 500 for PLC troubleshooting. On ABB 6400 robots with S4C controllers, performed rev-counter updates for individual axes and diagnosed pendant wiring, axis-drive, and controller-board problems.

FAULT INVESTIGATION

03 / APPROACH

How I approached it.

  1. Traced control logic and electrical faults using RSLinx and RSLogix 500.
  2. Identified sliced pendant wires associated with communication issues.
  3. Investigated an axis-specific short-circuit error involving a failing drive.
  4. Diagnosed a failed controller board that required board replacement; performed individual-axis rev-counter updates.
04 / OUTCOME

What the work delivered.

Identified wiring, axis-drive, and controller-board faults; performed individual-axis rev-counter updates.

DESIGN PERSPECTIVE

Effective troubleshooting follows the fault across system boundaries and narrows the diagnosis to a specific component or connection.
