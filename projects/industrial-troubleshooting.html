Brief

Production equipment issues can originate in wiring, controls, drives, or mechanical components. The symptom alone does not identify the source.

My work

Used RSLinx and RSLogix 500 for PLC troubleshooting. On ABB 6400 robots with S4C controllers, performed rev-counter updates for individual axes and diagnosed pendant wiring, axis-drive, and controller-board problems.

  • Traced control logic and electrical faults using RSLinx and RSLogix 500.
  • Identified sliced pendant wires associated with communication issues.
  • Investigated an axis-specific short-circuit error involving a failing drive.
  • Diagnosed a failed controller board that required board replacement; performed individual-axis rev-counter updates.

Engineering detail

Stop-pin actuation and follow-up

Worked with maintenance to diagnose shearing and loosening at a blaster stop-pin cylinder, installed replacement parts, and corrected additional issues during reassembly. Initial monitoring was encouraging, but later updates recorded mounting bolts backing out. The October 2025 updates still listed follow-up monitoring.

Chain drive losing timing

Traced flight-roll timing loss to a chain tensioner that was not maintaining tension. Selected a different tensioner style and waterjet-cut a mounting plate for the replacement arrangement.

Robot position recovery

Worked with a colleague on robot recalibration after a power outage, including program loading and manual jogging. Recalibrated a stack robot that had lost its position. Backup-battery replacement was listed as a next step.

Inspection-system integration

Supported a Keyence inspection project with waterjet-cut mounting hardware, mock-up checks, and production inspection support. This contribution was mechanical integration and test support; no inspection-accuracy or throughput result is claimed here.

Result

Identified wiring, axis-drive, and controller-board faults; performed individual-axis rev-counter updates.
