Conveyor-hook inspection fixture

Checked a supplier-built conveyor-hook fixture in CAD and at delivery, then helped trial marked sample hooks on the line.

Fixtures inspected; limited line trial completed

  • CAD fit review
  • Fixture inspection
  • Supplier coordination

Selected projects PDF ↗

Brief

Maintenance needed a repeatable way to check bent conveyor hooks. The fixture had to fit the hooks already in use on the line.

My work

I reviewed hook-to-fixture fit in CAD, communicated fit concerns to the supplier, coordinated reference hooks, and inspected the delivered fixtures.

  • Used CAD to check the hook against the existing fixture drawing.
  • Shared the fit concerns and coordinated reference hooks for the supplier, which fabricated the fixtures.
  • Checked the delivered fixtures and placed one set at the maintenance hook-bending station.
  • Worked with the line team to trial marked sample hooks and reported the result to the supplier.

Engineering detail

Scope of the line trial

The January update records a trial on marked samples. Delivery of the remaining hooks was still a next step.

Result

The fixtures were received and inspected. The January 2026 update documents a limited line trial of marked sample hooks and confirmation shared with the supplier.
