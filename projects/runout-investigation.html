Brief

Runout-related rejects on extrusion parts prompted an investigation into the part-holding arrangement.

My work

Developed and implemented a sleeve for the extrusion setup, then revised the holder design after a serviceability review.

  • Reviewed the runout issue with production and maintenance and developed a sleeve concept for the existing setup.
  • Measured the sleeve geometry and coordinated machining and installation as an interim correction.
  • Revised the holder after a serviceability review and prepared drawings for a hardware trial.

Engineering detail

Additional example: GM process study

A separate entry in my work record documents a GM process study in which I measured 20 marked axles through forging, extrusion, and straightening. No clear runout correlation was found. This is additional process-measurement experience, not evidence that established the sleeve design's root cause.

Serviceability review

Production and maintenance feedback changed the first holder concept. I revised the design and drawing before ordering trial parts. The drawing and CAD show design progress; they do not demonstrate a validated production result.

Result

After the sleeve was installed, the runout-related reject rate was below 2%. By the April 2026 update, two redesigned holder parts had been ordered; their hardware test was still a next step.
