PROJECT 03 / CVT / ECVT development

Bringing a CVT model to the test vehicle

Built a data-acquisition system to compare real drivetrain behavior with a CVT simulation.

DAQ built and compared with model
ENGINEERING AREAS

Test & validation / Design

TOOLS & METHODS
MATLABSimulinkData acquisitionSensorsModel comparison
01 / THE PROBLEM

What needed to change.

A CVT model needed to be compared with physical drivetrain behavior, using measurements that describe both transmission motion and rotational speeds.

02 / MY CONTRIBUTION

My part in the work.

Built a DAQ system to collect sheave linear travel, engine RPM, and wheel-speed RPM, and compared these measurements with a CVT model I developed.

03 / APPROACH

How I approached it.

  1. Developed a CVT model in MATLAB / Simulink.
  2. Built instrumentation to capture sheave displacement alongside engine and wheel rotational speeds.
  3. Compared measured signals with the model to assess agreement and investigate differences.
04 / OUTCOME

What the work delivered.

Produced a working measurement-to-model comparison across sheave travel, engine speed, and wheel speed.

DESIGN PERSPECTIVE

A simulation becomes more useful when its predictions can be compared with signals collected from the physical system.
