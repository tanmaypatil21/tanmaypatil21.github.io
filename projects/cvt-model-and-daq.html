Brief

A CVT model needed to be compared with physical drivetrain behavior, using measurements that describe both transmission motion and rotational speeds.

My work

Built a DAQ system to collect sheave linear travel, engine RPM, and wheel-speed RPM, and compared these measurements with a CVT model I developed.

  • Developed a CVT model in MATLAB / Simulink.
  • Built instrumentation to capture sheave displacement alongside engine and wheel rotational speeds.
  • Compared measured signals with the model to assess agreement and investigate differences.

Result

Produced a working measurement-to-model comparison across sheave travel, engine speed, and wheel speed.
