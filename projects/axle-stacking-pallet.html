Robot-compatible axle pallet

Brief

The pallet needed partitions to secure axles and accommodate the stacking robot's motion and part arrangements.

My work

Interpreted the robot stacking program and used AutoCAD and SolidWorks to evaluate overlapping stacking patterns and design the pallet.

  • Reviewed robot stacking patterns and axle configurations.
  • Used layout comparisons to establish partition spacing.
  • Developed a detailed CAD pallet design.

Result

Developed a robot-informed pallet layout with partitions intended to prevent axle rolling.
