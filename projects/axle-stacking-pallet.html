PROJECT 09 / Mid-West Forge

Mechanical design informed by robot motion

Used robot stacking patterns to develop a partitioned metal pallet for axle handling.

CAD design & program review
ENGINEERING AREAS

Design / Manufacturing

TOOLS & METHODS
AutoCADSolidWorksRobot program interpretationMaterial handling
01 / THE PROBLEM

What needed to change.

The pallet needed partitions to secure axles and accommodate the stacking robot's motion and part arrangements.

02 / MY CONTRIBUTION

My part in the work.

Interpreted the robot stacking program and used AutoCAD and SolidWorks to evaluate overlapping stacking patterns and design the pallet.

03 / APPROACH

How I approached it.

  1. Reviewed robot stacking patterns and axle configurations.
  2. Used layout comparisons to establish partition spacing.
  3. Developed a detailed CAD pallet design.
04 / OUTCOME

What the work delivered.

Developed a robot-informed pallet layout with partitions intended to prevent axle rolling.

DESIGN PERSPECTIVE

Mechanical fixtures work best when their geometry reflects the motion and constraints of the surrounding automation.
