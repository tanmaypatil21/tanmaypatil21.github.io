LVDT gauge-module redesign

Reconstructed an existing gauge module and revised its mechanical design around reliability concerns from the shop floor.

CAD and drawings complete; testing pending

Tools & methods

  • SolidWorks
  • PDM
  • Assembly design
  • Engineering drawings
  • BOM preparation
Selected projects PDF ↗

Brief

The existing gauge-module body needed mechanical changes while preserving compatibility with the rest of the assembly.

My work

Modeled the complete module, incorporated feedback from production and maintenance, and prepared controlled part and assembly drawings.

  • Reviewed the existing gauge-module issues with the people who operated and maintained it.
  • Built the component and assembly CAD to check how the revised parts fit together.
  • Released parts into PDM, completed manufacturing and assembly drawings, and ordered the parts.

Engineering detail

Mechanical scope

This case concerns the gauge-module body and assembly. It is separate from the EZTouch operator-screen migration and from earlier LVDT replacement or guard work.

A reviewable manufacturing handoff

The deliverables included component CAD, an assembly, part drawings, an assembly drawing, and purchasing information. Testing remains a separate step after the parts arrive.

Result

By the April 2026 update, CAD and drawings were complete and parts were ordered. Assembly testing and reliability results were still pending in the source record.
