Interlocking differential stand

Designed and fabricated an interlocking sheet-metal stand to display an assembled differential and axles.

Designed and fabricated

Tools & methods

  • SolidWorks
  • Sheet metal
  • Waterjet
  • DFM / DFA
Selected projects PDF ↗

Brief

The display needed to demonstrate how the company's axles fit into a working differential assembly.

My work

Created the stand in SolidWorks and used DFM / DFA principles for an interlocking aluminum sheet-metal design fabricated with waterjet cutting.

  • Designed around the differential and axle assembly.
  • Developed an interlocking stand suited to sheet-metal fabrication.
  • Used waterjet-cut aluminum parts to build the display.

Result

A fabricated display stand supported the assembled differential and axles.
