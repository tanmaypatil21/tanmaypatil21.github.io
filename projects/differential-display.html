PROJECT 08 / Mid-West Forge

A display built around the manufacturing process

Designed and fabricated an interlocking sheet-metal stand to display an assembled differential and axles.

Designed and fabricated
ENGINEERING AREAS

Design / Manufacturing

TOOLS & METHODS
SolidWorksSheet metalWaterjetDFM / DFA
01 / THE PROBLEM

What needed to change.

The display needed to demonstrate how the company's axles fit into a working differential assembly.

02 / MY CONTRIBUTION

My part in the work.

Created the stand in SolidWorks and used DFM / DFA principles for an interlocking aluminum sheet-metal design fabricated with waterjet cutting.

03 / APPROACH

How I approached it.

  1. Designed around the differential and axle assembly.
  2. Developed an interlocking stand suited to sheet-metal fabrication.
  3. Used waterjet-cut aluminum parts to build the display.
04 / OUTCOME

What the work delivered.

A fabricated display stand supported the assembled differential and axles.

DESIGN PERSPECTIVE

A straightforward design can demonstrate strong material, manufacturing, and assembly decisions.
